Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's warm surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a summer's breeze,
Which one to choose, where to be?
Tomorrow, Madrid's sunshine bright,
Aalborg's clouds, Úbeda's heat in sight,
But Madrid's warmth, it calls to me,
A gentle spring, for you and me.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, diferentes cielos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid cálido y sorprendente,
Úbeda calor, una brisa de verano,
¿Cuál elegir, dónde estar?
Mañana, el sol de Madrid brilla con fuerza,
Las nubes de Aalborg, el calor de Úbeda a la vista,
Pero el calor de Madrid, me llama a mí,
Una suave primavera, para ti y para mí.

This poem compares the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda, highlighting their differences. It suggests that Madrid's warm sunshine makes it the most appealing place to be tomorrow.
