Here is a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

Mañana, ¿dónde ir?
Tomorrow, where to go?
Aalborg, con viento y frío,
Aalborg, with wind and cold,
no es el mejor lugar, no.
is not the best place, no.

Madrid, con sol y calor,
Madrid, with sun and heat,
es una buena opción, sí.
is a good option, yes.

Úbeda, con calor y sol,
Úbeda, with heat and sun,
es el lugar perfecto, ¡vamos!
is the perfect place, let's go!

Translation note: I've kept the same rhythm and meter in both languages to make it easy to read and understand. The poem suggests that Úbeda is the nicest place to be tomorrow due to its warm and sunny weather.
