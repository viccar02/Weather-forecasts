Here's a short weather poem in English and Spanish, comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's skies are gray and cold,
Madrid's sun shines bright and bold,
Úbeda's heat waves rise so high,
Choose Madrid, and let your spirit fly.

Spanish:
Los cielos de Aalborg son grises y fríos,
El sol de Madrid brilla con fuerza y orgullo,
Las olas de calor de Úbeda suben tan alto,
Elige Madrid, y deja volar tu espíritu.

This poem describes the differences in weather between the three locations and suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be, with its sunny and bold weather.
