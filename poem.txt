Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is proudly displayed,
Úbeda's heat is hard to bear,
Which city would you choose to share?

Aalborg's breeze is cool and bright,
Madrid's sun shines with gentle light,
Úbeda's flames burn with fiery might,
Where would you go for a peaceful night?

Mañana, ¿dónde te gustaría estar?
Aalborg's calm, Madrid's charm, or Úbeda's star?
Choose wisely, for the weather's sway,
Will make your day, come what may.

(Translation:
Tomorrow, where would you like to be?
Aalborg's calm, Madrid's charm, or Úbeda's star?
Choose wisely, for the weather's influence,
Will make your day, come what may.)

Note: The poem suggests that Aalborg is cool and calm, Madrid is warm and charming, and Úbeda is hot and fiery. The final line asks the reader to choose which city they would prefer to be in, considering the weather.
