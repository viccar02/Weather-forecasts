Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's warmth, a sunny delight,
Where to be tomorrow, a pleasant sight.
Madrid's heat, a summer's dream,
Úbeda's breeze, a gentle theme,
Aalborg's wind, a chilly breeze,
Tomorrow, Madrid's warmth, if you please.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda cálida, un deleite soleado,
¿Dónde estar mañana, un espectáculo agradable?
El calor de Madrid, un sueño de verano,
La brisa de Úbeda, un tema suave,
El viento de Aalborg, una brisa fría,
Mañana, el calor de Madrid, si lo deseas.

Note: The poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, considering the warmer temperatures and pleasant weather. However, please keep in mind that this is a poetic interpretation and not a real-time weather forecast.
