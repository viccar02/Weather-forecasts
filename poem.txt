Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is nicely made,
Úbeda's heat starts to rise high,
Choose Madrid, with a gentle sky.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid está bien hecho,
El calor de Úbeda comienza a subir alto,
Elige Madrid, con un cielo suave.

Note: This poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be, with a gentle sky and pleasant warmth, compared to Aalborg's chill and Úbeda's intense heat.
