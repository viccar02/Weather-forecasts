Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a burning flame,
Which one to choose, where to proclaim?
Tomorrow's forecast, a gentle breeze,
In Aalborg, calm, in Madrid, ease,
Úbeda's sun, a warm delight,
Perhaps Madrid, a pleasant sight.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda calor, llama ardiente,
¿Cuál elegir, dónde proclamar?
El pronóstico de mañana, brisa suave,
En Aalborg, calma, en Madrid, facil,
El sol de Úbeda, delicia cálida,
Quizás Madrid, vista agradable.

Note: This poem is a simplified comparison of the weather in the three cities, and is not meant to be a precise forecast. The actual weather conditions may vary.
