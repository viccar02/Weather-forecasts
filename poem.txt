Here's a short bilingual weather poem comparing Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda shines with sunny might,
A perfect day to bask in light.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecerse,
El calor de Madrid se hace suavemente,
Úbeda brilla con esplendor solar,
Un día perfecto para disfrutar de la luz.

English:
But where to go tomorrow, you ask?
Madrid's heat will surely last,
Aalborg's breeze will gently blow,
Úbeda's sunshine will make it glow.

Spanish:
Pero ¿dónde ir mañana, preguntas?
El calor de Madrid durará seguro,
La brisa de Aalborg soplará suavemente,
El sol de Úbeda lo hará brillar.

Note: The poem is based on the general weather trends in each location, with Aalborg being cooler, Madrid being warm, and Úbeda being sunny. The poem suggests that Úbeda might be the nicest place to be tomorrow, with its sunny weather.
