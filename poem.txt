English:

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid y la sombra de Úbeda,
Mañana, ¿dónde deambular?
La luz de Madrid me llama a su hogar.

Note: This poem is based on the general trend of the weather data provided, with Aalborg being the coldest, Madrid being the warmest, and Úbeda having a mix of both. The poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow due to its warm and sunny weather.
