Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's warmth, a sunny delight,
Which one to choose, for a pleasant night?
Tomorrow, Madrid's the place to be,
With warm sunshine, and a gentle breeze.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda cálida, un delicioso sol,
¿Cuál elegir, para una noche placentera?
Mañana, Madrid es el lugar para estar,
Con sol cálido, y una brisa suave.

Note: The poem is based on the general weather trends in the given data, and is not specific to a particular date. Madrid is suggested as the nicest place to be tomorrow, due to its warm and sunny weather.
