Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

El clima varía en cada lugar,
The climate varies in each place,
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is nicely displayed.
Úbeda's heat is hard to bear,
But sunshine makes our spirits share.

Mañana, ¿dónde estaría mejor?
Tomorrow, where would it be best?
Aalborg's mild and gentle breeze,
Madrid's warmth with sunny ease,
Úbeda's heat with clear blue skies,
Choose your place, and sunny surprise.

Note: The poem is a short and creative comparison of the weather in the three locations, suggesting that the best place to be tomorrow would depend on individual preferences for temperature and weather conditions.
