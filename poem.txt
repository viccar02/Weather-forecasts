Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's skies are gray and cold,
Madrid shines with warmth to behold.
Úbeda's sunbeams bright and bold,
Which city would you choose to unfold?
Tomorrow, Madrid's the place to be,
With mild breeze and warm destiny.

Spanish:
Los cielos de Aalborg están grises y fríos,
Madrid brilla con calor que impresiona.
Los rayos de sol de Úbeda son brillantes y fuertes,
¿Qué ciudad elegirías para desplegar?
Mañana, Madrid es el lugar donde estar,
Con brisa suave y destino cálido y agradable.

Note: The poem is based on the general weather trend of the three locations, with Madrid having the most pleasant weather, especially in the spring season.
