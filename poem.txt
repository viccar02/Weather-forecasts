Here's a short weather poem in English and Spanish, comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda's sunshine bright and bold,
Choose Madrid, where warmth never grows old.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid se crea con suavidad,
La luz solar de Úbeda brilla con fuerza,
Elige Madrid, donde el calor nunca envejece.

Note: This poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, considering the overall warmth and sunshine in the city.
