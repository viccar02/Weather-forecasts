Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda shines with sunny rays,
Tomorrow, Madrid's the nicest place to stay.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid se hace suavemente,
Úbeda brilla con rayos de sol,
Mañana, Madrid es el lugar más agradable para quedarse.

This poem compares the weather in the three locations, highlighting the chill of Aalborg, the warmth of Madrid, and the sunny rays of Úbeda. It suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, given its gentle warmth.
