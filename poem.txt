Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's warmth, a gentle breeze,
Which one to choose, where to be?

Madrid's sunshine, bright and bold,
Úbeda's heat, a story to be told,
Aalborg's winds, a cooler tale,
Tomorrow, Madrid's the place to hail.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda cálida, brisa suave,
¿Cuál elegir, dónde estar?

Madrid sol brillante, audaz,
Úbeda calor, historia que contar,
Aalborg vientos, relato fresco,
Mañana, Madrid es el lugar más bello.
