Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda's sun shines bright and bold,
Tomorrow, Madrid's warmth to hold.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid se crea suavemente,
El sol de Úbeda brilla con fuerza y valentía,
Mañana, el calor de Madrid nos espera.

This poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, with its gentle warmth and sunshine. Aalborg is still a bit chilly, while Úbeda's sun is shining bright, but Madrid's warmth is the most appealing.
