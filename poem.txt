Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda's heat starts to arise,
Which city would you choose to realize?
Tomorrow's forecast, a gentle breeze,
Madrid's sunshine brings us to our knees,
A perfect day to soak up the light,
In Madrid, all will be alright.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecerse,
El calor de Madrid se hace sentir con suavidad,
El calor de Úbeda comienza a surgir,
¿Qué ciudad elegirías para realizar?
El pronóstico del mañana, una brisa suave,
La lluvia de sol de Madrid nos hace rendirnos,
Un día perfecto para absorber la luz,
En Madrid, todo estará bien.

Note: The poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, based on the forecast of gentle sunshine and warm temperatures.
