Here is a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a sunny delight,
Which one to choose, for a pleasant night?
Tomorrow, let's head to Madrid's gate,
Warm and sunny, a perfect state.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg fresco, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda caluroso, un deleite solar,
¿Cuál elegir, para una noche agradable?
Mañana, vayamos a la puerta de Madrid,
Cálido y soleado, un estado perfecto.

Note: The poem is based on the general trend of the weather data provided, and not on a specific date. Madrid is suggested as the best option for tomorrow due to its warm and sunny weather.
