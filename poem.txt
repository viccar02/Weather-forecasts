Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a burning flame,
Each unique, yet all the same.
Tomorrow, where to be?
Perhaps Madrid, warm and free,
Aalborg's winds, Úbeda's heat,
Madrid's the spot, to find a treat.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda calor, llama ardiente,
Cada uno único, yet todos iguales.
Mañana, dónde estar?
Quizás Madrid, cálido y libre,
Vientos de Aalborg, calor de Úbeda,
Madrid es el lugar, para encontrar un placer.

This poem compares the weather in the three cities, highlighting their unique characteristics. Aalborg is portrayed as chilly, Madrid as surprising and warm, and Úbeda as hot. The poem suggests that Madrid might be the most pleasant place to be tomorrow, with its warm and free atmosphere.
