Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

**English:**
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a sunny delight,
Which one to choose, for a pleasant night?
Aalborg's winds, a gentle breeze,
Madrid's warmth, a sunny tease,
Úbeda's sun, a fiery blaze,
Tomorrow, Madrid's the perfect phase.

**Spanish:**
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg fresco, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda calor, un deleite soleado,
¿Cuál elegir, para una noche agradable?
Los vientos de Aalborg, una brisa suave,
El calor de Madrid, un sol tentador,
El sol de Úbeda, una llamarada,
Mañana, Madrid es la fase perfecta.

Note: The poem is based on the general weather trends in each city, with Aalborg being cooler, Madrid being warmer, and Úbeda being the hottest. The poem suggests that Madrid would be the most pleasant city to be in tomorrow, considering its warm and sunny weather.
