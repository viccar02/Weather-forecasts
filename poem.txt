Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

Aalborg, ciudad del norte frío,
Madrid y Úbeda, con calor que brilla.
Aalborg's chill begins to unfold,
While Madrid and Úbeda shine with heat to hold.

La temperatura en Aalborg es baja y gris,
En Madrid y Úbeda, el sol brilla con fuerza y risa.
Aalborg's temperature is low and gray,
In Madrid and Úbeda, the sun shines bright each day.

Mañana, ¿dónde estaría mejor?
Perhaps in Úbeda, with its warm and sunny weather to favor.
Tomorrow, where would it be nice to be?
Maybe in Úbeda, with its warm and sunny weather, a sight to see.

Note: I've taken some creative liberties with the poem to make it more engaging and easy to read, while still conveying the general idea of comparing the weather in the three locations.
