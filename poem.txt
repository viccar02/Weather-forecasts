Here is a short weather poem in English and Spanish, comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a summer's sway,
Tomorrow, Úbeda's the best way.
Warm and sunny, a perfect day,
Leave Aalborg's clouds, Madrid's grey,
And head to Úbeda, where heat does stay.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda calurosa, un verano que se balancea,
Mañana, Úbeda es el mejor camino.
Cálido y soleado, un día perfecto,
Deja las nubes de Aalborg, el gris de Madrid,
Y dirigete a Úbeda, donde el calor se queda.

This poem suggests that Úbeda would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, with its warm and sunny weather, compared to Aalborg's chill and Madrid's potential grey skies.
