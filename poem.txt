Here's a short weather poem in English and Spanish:

**English**
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda's heat starts to shine bright,
Choose Madrid for a pleasant night.

**Spanish**
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid se forma con suavidad,
El calor de Úbeda comienza a brillar con fuerza,
Elige Madrid para una noche agradable.

Note: The poem compares the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda, suggesting that Madrid would be the most pleasant place to be tomorrow due to its mild and warm temperatures.
