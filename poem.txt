Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

Hoy y mañana, 
el clima varía, 
en Aalborg, frío y viento, 
en Madrid, sol y calor, 
en Úbeda, temperatura agradable.

Tomorrow's forecast shows,
Aalborg's chilly, windy blows,
Madrid's sunny, warm and bright,
Úbeda's mild, a pleasant sight.

Mañana, ¿dónde estaría bien?
En Madrid, con sol y calor,
o en Úbeda, con temperatura suave,
pero no en Aalborg, con viento y frío.

Where would be nice to be tomorrow?
In Madrid, with sun and warmth,
or in Úbeda, with gentle temperature,
but not in Aalborg, with wind and cold.

(Note: The poem is written in a way that the English and Spanish versions are not literal translations of each other, but rather an attempt to convey the same message and atmosphere in both languages.)
