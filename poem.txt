Here's a short weather poem in English and Spanish, comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a sunny glow,
Each one unique, as the winds do blow.
Tomorrow's forecast, a gentle breeze,
Úbeda's warmth, a pleasant tease,
Leave your coat, in Aalborg's grey,
And head to Úbeda, for a sunny day.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, cielos distintos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda calor, un resplandor soleado,
Cada uno único, como los vientos que soplan.
El pronóstico de mañana, una brisa suave,
El calor de Úbeda, un atractivo placentero,
Deja tu abrigo, en el gris de Aalborg,
Y dirígete a Úbeda, para un día soleado.

Note: The poem suggests that Úbeda would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, due to its warm and sunny weather.
