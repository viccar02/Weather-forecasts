Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda's heat starts to rise high,
Each city's charm catches the eye.
Tomorrow, Madrid's sunny day,
Would be the nicest place to stay.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid se hace suavemente,
El calor de Úbeda comienza a subir alto,
Cada ciudad tiene un encanto que atrapa la vista.
Mañana, el día soleado de Madrid,
Sería el lugar más agradable para quedarse.

Note: This poem is a brief comparison of the three cities' weather, highlighting the unique characteristics of each place. It suggests that Madrid would be the most pleasant destination for the next day, given its sunny weather.
