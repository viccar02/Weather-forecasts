Úbeda's sun beats down with glee,
A perfect day to be in Spain, you'll see.

Mañana, Madrid's the place to be,
With warm sunshine and a gentle sea,
Leave Aalborg's chill and Úbeda's rain,
And bask in Madrid's sunny Spain.
