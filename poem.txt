Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

English:
Three cities, different skies,
Aalborg's chill, Madrid's surprise,
Úbeda's heat, a sunny fate,
Which one to choose, don't be late.
Tomorrow's forecast, a gentle breeze,
Madrid's warmth, a perfect tease,
Leave Aalborg's clouds, and Úbeda's heat,
Madrid's the place, where love will meet.

Spanish:
Tres ciudades, diferentes cielos,
Aalborg frío, Madrid sorprendente,
Úbeda caluroso, un destino soleado,
¿Cuál elegir, no te demores?
El pronóstico de mañana, una brisa suave,
El calor de Madrid, un placer perfecto,
Deja las nubes de Aalborg, y el calor de Úbeda,
Madrid es el lugar, donde el amor se encuentra.

Note: The poem is based on the general weather trends in each city, with Aalborg being the coolest, Madrid being mild and sunny, and Úbeda being the hottest. The poem suggests that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow, with its warm and gentle weather.
