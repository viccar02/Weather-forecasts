Here is a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda:

Tomorrow's forecast, a tale to tell,
Mañana's pronóstico, una historia que contar.
Aalborg's chill, with winds so strong,
Aalborg frío, con vientos tan fuertes.

Madrid's warmth, a gentle breeze,
Madrid cálido, con una brisa suave.
Úbeda's heat, a sun-kissed day,
Úbeda calor, un día besado por el sol.

If I had to choose, I'd say with glee,
Si tuviera que elegir, diría con alegría.
Madrid's the place, where I'd love to be,
Madrid es el lugar, donde me encantaría estar.

Note: I've taken some creative liberties with the poem to make it more engaging and easy to read, while still conveying the general idea of comparing the weather in the three locations.
