Here's a short bilingual poem comparing the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda, suggesting where it would be nicest to be tomorrow:

**English:**
Aalborg's chill begins to rise,
Madrid's warmth touches sunny skies.
Úbeda's heat, a summer's sway,
Which city to choose, come what may?

Tomorrow, Madrid's gentle breeze,
Invites us to stroll with ease.
So let's head to Madrid's delight,
And bask in sunshine, warm and bright.

**Spanish:**
Aalborg comienza a calentar,
Madrid toca el cielo soleado con su calor.
Úbeda's calor, un verano suave,
¿Qué ciudad elegir, qué hacer?

Mañana, la brisa suave de Madrid,
Nos invita a pasear con facilidad.
Así que vámonos a la delicia de Madrid,
Y bañémonos en sol, cálido y radiante.
