Here's a short weather poem in English and Spanish:

English:
Aalborg's chill begins to fade,
Madrid's warmth is gently made,
Úbeda's sunshine beams so bright,
Tomorrow, Madrid's the nicest sight.

Spanish:
El frío de Aalborg comienza a desvanecer,
El calor de Madrid se forma con suavidad,
La luz del sol de Úbeda brilla con fuerza,
Mañana, Madrid es el lugar más agradable.

Note: This poem compares the weather in Aalborg, Madrid, and Úbeda, describing the differences and suggesting that Madrid would be the nicest place to be tomorrow.
